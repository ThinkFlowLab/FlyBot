A room's invite link now has the endpoints the Members card works through: read the live link, mint one, withdraw it, and knock at a link someone else handed you — first for the preview, then again with `accept: true` when Join is clicked ([#876](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/876)). The knock leaves from one of your own Bots and names the Bot to knock on from the link, never from the request body; a link into a room on another FlyBot, a bare ticket, and a ticket outside the 16–64 character shape are each refused in one sentence and send nothing.
