A group created from the sidebar now names the person who invited a friend's Bot, not "a friend": the social plugin keeps the owner-level name and home on each Bot's member record (from `whoami`), and the share envelope reads them when no human participant is in the room. A room that does have a human participant still names that human, and a room mirrored from an inbound share forwards its original inviter instead of this home's owner ([#848](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/848)).
