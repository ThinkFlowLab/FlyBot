A human message in a Face-to-face room (or any group) that names a friend's Bot already in that room is delivered as that human in the room. It is no longer relayed as Chief, so Chief's missing grant cannot block it ([#143](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/143)). A Chief-originated send to an ungranted friend Bot is still refused. Failed 1:1 sends in Friends' Bots now say one short English line with a next step, not a raw `Bot Social send…` preview.
