A diagnostics report from a computer whose DSH home is nested deeply no longer cuts the end off the `Read-only` row: the row used to stop at `… close the other one and` because the long home filled its 200 characters. A home too deep for that bound now gives up its middle instead (`~/.local/share/deepseek-harness/teams/flybot-dev` reads `~/.local/…/flybot-dev`), keeping the last level the sentence names and the advice after it whole.
