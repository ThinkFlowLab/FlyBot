The Feedback page's error count now divides by the whole log, not by the rows on screen: a log of sixty errors reads `Showing 8 of 60` folded and `Showing 50 of 60` open, instead of `Showing 8 of 50` and `Showing all 50` under a `Recent errors · 60` title. The count line and the title now report the same number, so ten errors are no longer missing from a page that claims to hold all of them. See [#2932](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2932).
