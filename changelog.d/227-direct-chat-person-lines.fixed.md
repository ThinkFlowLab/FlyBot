In a direct chat with a friend's Bot, the line you type is yours: it goes out at once as your own message (no approval card labelled `Chief → …`, no "waits for your approval" reply), and the friend sees it from you, with your name, not from your Chief. A Bot's message can never pass as yours. Approving or rejecting a held Bot line clears the chat's wait badge at once.
