A turn that DSH ended at the model's output limit is no longer reported as a normal finish or as a member that chose silence: the activity feed marks `turn.end` with `limited: true`, the ended-turn view keeps the turn (with `silent` and the streak when it wrote nothing, and its own sentence in the chat instead of "could not run"), and a group member cut off before it answered is recorded as "no answer" instead of a pass ([#2679](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2679)).
