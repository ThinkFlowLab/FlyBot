The Workflow stage no longer reads the store from a window nobody is looking at: the 2 s poll kept asking the host for `workflow/state` — the whole store, including the widened held-task page — for as long as a run was live or a read error was showing, so a minimised app or a tab behind another kept the host busy every tick, and coming back showed rows up to 2 s stale. The poll now follows the page: a hidden window arms no interval at all, returning clears and re-arms it and then reads once at once, and the visible cadence stays 2000 ms ([#3361](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/3361)).
