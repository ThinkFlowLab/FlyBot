The enqueue guard no longer reads the closing line of our own verdict request — a first-person offer like "say so on the PR and I will hold it" — as a stop request. A real request still stops it: "please hold it", "do not merge this tip", and a first-person hold with a condition attached ("I will hold it until the second reader signs off"). See [#3083](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/3083).
