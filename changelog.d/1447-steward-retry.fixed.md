A single throttled context-steward call no longer moves the conversation on to a fresh part: a rate limit, a timeout or a 5xx on that one request is now retried (up to three times, honouring the retry delay the provider names when it names one), and if it still fails below the point where the part must move, the round is spent instead of the part and the next round asks again only after a cooldown. Over that point, and for a refusal that retrying cannot change, the part still moves on, and the handoff now records the real reason instead of the default "the previous part grew long" ([#1447](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1447)).
