Creating or editing a group that holds a friend's Bot and one of your own Bots that is not shared with that friend no longer ends in the bare red line "Only a Bot you granted (or one that joined with the room code) may be in a room with friends' Bots". The refusal now says which Bot is not shared with whom and why (a group with a friend's Bot is sent on to the friend's home), and the new-chat screen offers **Copy invite link for {Bot}** (the friend accepts it to share that Bot) or **Leave {Bot} out**. Nothing is shared by the click; the rule itself (grants are per Bot, default deny) is unchanged. If the clipboard is unavailable or refuses the copy, the card no longer says the link was copied: it shows the link in a field to copy by hand.
