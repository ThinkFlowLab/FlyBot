Settings → Workflows no longer cuts off the left side of a graph that is too wide for the canvas: below the half-size floor the leftover width is reserved on the left of the stage instead of spilling out of both edges, so the leftmost card and the dashed frame are drawn where they can be seen and the rest scrolls to the right — a scroller cannot scroll left of zero, which is why those pixels could not be brought back before ([#1769](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1769)).
