`read_group_chat`'s `newest N of M` no longer trusts a booking list shorter than the room's own count: a room that kept only its booking count and recorded one number afterwards reported fewer lines than its window holds (`newest 30 of 206` where 196 are there), because the unrecorded bookings were counted as if every one sat inside the window. Such a room's placements are unknown, so it keeps the room-wide discount. ([#3046](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/3046))
