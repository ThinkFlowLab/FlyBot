The timeline's OTLP exporter no longer follows a redirect from the collector: a `307` reply that points at another origin is an export failure (warned, not counted as delivered, retried on the next flush) instead of a replay of `otlpHeaders` and the span payload at a host the operator never named. A normal `200` still counts as delivered ([#2687](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2687)).
