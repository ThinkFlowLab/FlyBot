A group round's closing report no longer folds a member that was addressed with `@` and never answered into the same sentence as a member that read the line and passed: the two now get separate sentences, so "he had nothing to add" can be told from "he was called and said nothing". A member the line did not name still appears under `Stayed silent:` ([#913](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/913)).
