A group chat's details panel now lists the scheduled tasks that room owns. Before, a task could target a group chat — the new-task form offered `Group chats`, and Settings grouped tasks by room — but the room itself showed nothing, so "is the 9am task on this room still there?" meant leaving the room for Settings. The panel draws the three newest of the room's tasks, a title carrying the real count and `N more not shown` past three, and only tasks that carry this room's `roomId` and no `botId`: a Bot's task and another room's task stay out. Rows open the settings list, a room with no tasks draws nothing at all, and a face-to-face room gets no such section ([#2039](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2039)).
