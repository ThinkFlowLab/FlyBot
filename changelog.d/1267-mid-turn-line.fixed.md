A line sent into a group chat while a member is speaking now reaches that member inside its turn: the round watches the same Session queue it drains, logs the line at once and steers it into the speaker's running turn with a header saying it arrived after the turn started, so the member's next step answers it instead of reading it after a long turn as if it had been there all along. The line leaves the relay queue, so it is neither logged nor answered twice. The one exception is an explicit stop (停, 停下, 停止, 别做了, 不要做了, stop, stop it): the speaking slot is marked stopped by the user, its turn is cancelled and its answer is not posted, and the line stays queued to be answered as a fresh line by the room ([#1267](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1267)).
