`read_own_chat({ part: N })` no longer calls the current part "in your context" after a checkpoint condensed it. When the part carries a `[Checkpoint N]`, the answer names the checkpoint, shows the newest messages that are still in context, and points at the words search for the rest; without a checkpoint, and for an ended or missing part, the answer is unchanged. See [#2774](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2774).
