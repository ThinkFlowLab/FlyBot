Pressing **Stop** while a requests-per-window `429` had a Bot's answer waiting on a backoff timer now drops that wait: a stopped round is not asked again, so it costs no model call and no tools, and the answer it would have produced is no longer thrown away afterwards. A stop that lands after the retry already went out still cancels the running turn, a `429` with no stop retries exactly as before, and a retry a newer turn in the same Session is already waiting on is left alone ([#1679](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1679)).
