The sidebar no longer draws a separator for a section that has nothing to show: the `Archived (N)` group, and the reorganisation notice beside it, left an empty wrapper behind that drew a 1 px line with 8 px of margin over nothing, so a fresh install showed two rules with nothing between them ([#818](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/818)). The archived section also reads the roster the sidebar already has instead of fetching it a second time on its own 10 s timer, so a tick is one roster read rather than two; an expanded group stays expanded across a tick.
