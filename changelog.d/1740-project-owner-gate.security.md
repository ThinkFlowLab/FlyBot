The four Project endpoints that delete a Project or expose and change its assistant memory — `project-delete`, `project-memory`, `project-memory-write` and `project-assistant-owner` — now ask the Project's owner first: a signed-in member who does not own the Project gets `bot/forbidden` and the Project, its private memory and the other owner's digest settings are unchanged, instead of a deletion, a read of another member's memory, a write into the digest's source, or a rewrite of another owner's quiet window and opt-out ([#1740](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1740)).
