The busy-ack bubble no longer repeats one canned sentence. While a Bot is working, a new message gets a short one-line ack composed on the spot from what you wrote and what the Bot is doing (e.g. 「看到你说「帮我改周报」了，我正在跑命令，弄完马上接上。」), in the language of your message, with varied wording that never repeats the previous ack — still instant, no extra model call ([#1629](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1629)).
