A workflow store that parses but holds an entry that is not a workflow now answers `store-unreadable` instead of throwing out of every read of `workflow/state`: the panel gets the repair hint, and the damaged bytes are kept as `workflows.json.broken-<stamp>` before the next write, instead of a raw `TypeError` escaping each render ([#2736](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2736)).
