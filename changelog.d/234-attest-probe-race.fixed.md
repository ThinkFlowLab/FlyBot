Face rooms no longer fall back to the forgeable `FLYBOT-FACE-HUMAN` envelope while the Bot Social feature probe is still in flight or just failed: concurrent room polls now await the one shared probe, and a probe that got no answer (server unreachable, or an older server without the platform endpoint) is retried after a minute instead of pinning the legacy behaviour for ten.
