A Bot condenses its conversation earlier: the steward's check line is now 45% of the model's usable window instead of 80%. Every call re-sends the fixed block and the conversation is most of each prompt, so condensing sooner cuts the prompt of a long turn by about a quarter; the cost is that a checkpoint arrives sooner and a condensed conversation keeps less detail. `checkRatio` still overrides it in the profile ([#1233](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1233)).
