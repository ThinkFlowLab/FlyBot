A workflow step the host refuses is no longer counted as delivered. When `fb.bot.send` answers `deny` (an edge outside the tree, a social limit, a middleware that threw) or `hold` (the message waits in the owner's approval queue), the step now stays unmarked and the next settle sends it again to the same Bot, with one line in the log naming which of the two refused it; an `allow` is unchanged. ([#1407](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1407))
