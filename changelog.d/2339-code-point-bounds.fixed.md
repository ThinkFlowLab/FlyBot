The memory-review user line and the room/board one-line fields still judged their limit in UTF-16 units while they cut in code points, so a line of characters outside the BMP was cut while it was already under its own limit: a 1,497-character user line reached the review as 1,497 emoji under a 3,000-character bound and lost about 1,400 of them, and a 40-character holder name or an 80-character card title came back at 19 and 39. Both bounds now judge with `fits()` in `core/src/shared/text-boundary.js`, with the maxima and the cuts unchanged ([#2339](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2339)).
