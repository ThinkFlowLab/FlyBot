Face-to-face rooms: a plain line (no @) now gets one answer. Each Bot passes by default, the Bot that spoke last is asked first for a follow-up, and the first Bot that answers ends the round: the Bots after it are not asked, so you no longer get three similar summaries. A line to everyone ("大家介绍一下自己", "你们…") still gets a short answer from each, and if every Bot passes on your own line the first Bot answers. `@Name` and `@all` are unchanged, replies' own `@`s start nothing, and approval and Auto-send rules are unchanged.
