A Bot turn now carries its own outcome to the sender instead of leaving it in the wording: an answer says `failure: false`, a turn stopped before it answered says `failure: true` with the stop notice as its reason, and a failed one keeps its failure notice — so a workflow step classifies the step by that marker, a stop notice can no longer advance a run, and an answer that happens to quote a failure sentence can no longer fail one ([#2742](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2742)).
