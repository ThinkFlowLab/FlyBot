A read-only window no longer draws the write controls on a local Bot's Sharing card or on a local group's Members card: making an invite link, sharing a Bot with a friend, People with access, Create link and Withdraw are all writes that the host refuses there, so each card says why the window cannot write instead of offering a button that can only fail ([#2418](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2418)).
