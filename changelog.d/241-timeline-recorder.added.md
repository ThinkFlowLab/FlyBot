New plugin `@flybot/timeline` (part 1 of [#241](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/241)): it records Bot turns, tool calls and Bot-to-Bot deliveries from the activity feed into a capped append-only trace (about 3000 spans or 24 hours, one writer, fsync and rename), and answers `flybot/timeline/window` with lanes, turns, request-to-reply latencies, a communication map and per-Bot utilization. Only ids, times, outcomes and cleaned names are stored, never message text, tool arguments or output. The panel follows in the next change.
