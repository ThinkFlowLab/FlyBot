The label-hygiene sweep no longer stops at the first label it cannot remove. A `404` on one DELETE means that label was already gone and is skipped; any other refusal — including the `422` that the live `#1356`/`#1359` runs returned, with the label still on the item — is collected together with the response body the request answered, the items behind it are still cleaned, and the run exits non-zero at the end naming what failed. The read-back after the sweep stays the criterion. ([#1418](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1418))
