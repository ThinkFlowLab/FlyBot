A room board's rail counted only the newest page of the room's finished cards, so a Bot whose four cards were all still open read `0/1` on a board that also held one finished card, and the numbers disagreed with the cards drawn in the column beside them. The rail now counts the room's active cards as well, so the same Bot reads `1/4` and every Bot on the page reads the same total ([#2063](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2063)).
