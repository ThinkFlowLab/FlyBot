A Project whose assistant was created while DSH automation tasks were off kept `error: "Automation tasks are off in DSH"` forever: switching automation back on left Scheduled tasks empty, and calling `project-create` again answered `created:false` without scheduling ([#1751](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1751)). The assistant now reconciles every Project whose assistant exists without a `scheduleId` — when the schedule service comes online and on a repeated `project-create` — writing the 09:00 Asia/Shanghai digest once and clearing the recorded error.
