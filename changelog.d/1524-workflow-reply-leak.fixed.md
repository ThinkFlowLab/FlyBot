**Workflow ephemeral node Bot replies no longer leak into persistent Bot chats.** Previously, ephemeral workflow node Bot replies (e.g., Review / Verify / Rework results) could appear as queued `[Reply from …]` messages in Chief or other persistent Bot conversations, especially when deliveries bypassed the send middleware (deferred, resumed, or after workflow release). The delivery gate in `hooks.js` now denies ALL bot-to-bot deliveries from ephemeral bots based on the durable `bot.ephemeral` flag, regardless of gating or workflow ownership state. Ephemeral Bot outputs stay strictly on the workflow run surface. (#1524)
