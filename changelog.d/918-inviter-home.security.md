A shared room no longer takes an inviter's handle from another home as that inviter's signature: a line that presents the inviter handle but arrives from a different home is kept and shown under the Bot that delivered it, marked unverified, instead of being signed as the inviter ([#918](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/918)). Only a `join` records which home invited a room — a line can no longer rewrite it, which is what made the signature check agree with itself.
