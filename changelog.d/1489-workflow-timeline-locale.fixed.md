The Workflow stage timeline now follows the shell language — task assigned, queued, workflow created, paused, resumed, stopped, the failure, hand-off and hand-off-retry lines, the deadline timeout, the retry and recovery frames, the parallel start/join and done frames, reset, and the canned Delivery replies read English in an English shell and Chinese in a Chinese one; each row keeps its Chinese text as the fallback for timelines written before the key existed.
