A read-only window's group overview in the details drawer now draws no write control: the group's name is plain text rather than a rename button, an invited share no longer offers **Remove my Bot**, and a shared room's "Waiting for your approval" card keeps its headline and rows but drops **Approve** and **Reject**, with the host's own sentence in the single `role="status"` line at the top of the page ([#2400](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2400)). A writable window keeps every control unchanged.
