The title of a group without a chosen name follows its seats now ([#1995](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1995)): a friend's Bot invited to such a group appears in its name and in the group's Session title the moment the seat exists, and a seat that leaves drops out of both instead of staying in `state.json` until a restart. Before this, only a settings save or a renamed Bot recomputed that name, so inviting or removing a guest left the title reading the old set of people while the roster had already changed. A group created together with a guest was opened under the narrow name — `You, Chief A, Chief B`, with the guest missing — and was corrected in `state.json` without ever reaching the Session the user reads. A group with a chosen name keeps it.
