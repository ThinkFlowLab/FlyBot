A workflow assignment's brief is now the caller's to clear. An explicit `brief` replaces the facts cached on the lane, and `brief: ''` clears them instead of falling back to the old text; deleting a workflow drops the facts it cached, so a later assignment carries nothing from it and records no handoff naming a lane that no longer exists ([#2475](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2475)).
