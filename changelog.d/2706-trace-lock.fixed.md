A timeline window that opens while another window is writing the trace no longer loses every span for the rest of its life: it asks for the writer lock again when it has something to write, begins persisting as soon as the other window closes, and keeps the segments already on disk. Until the lock is free a flush that wrote nothing answers `false` and names the reason once, instead of reporting success for a dropped span. See [#2706](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2706).
