`ask_user` now enforces the two-to-four answers its own card promises: a question with one option, or with more than four, is refused with `Give two to four answers.` and no card is stored, instead of accepting a single "choice" or silently dropping a seventh option (labels dropped as empty are not counted, so five given with two blanks is a three-button card and passes). Two to four options produce the same card and the same answer string as before. See [#2771](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2771).
