The Tree panel's reorganization notice no longer asks the host for its report from a window nobody is looking at: the card read `tree/reorg` every 30 s for as long as it was mounted, so a minimised app or a tab behind another kept the host busy on that read, and coming back could show a report up to 30 s old. The read now follows the page — a hidden window arms no timer, returning clears and re-arms it and then reads once at once, and unmounting leaves neither the timer nor the listener behind ([#3371](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/3371)).
