A decision or a meeting archive no longer stores half of an emoji ([#1912](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1912)): the host's one `cut` helper stops a bounded prefix before the surrogate pair it would split, and every archive writer now uses it instead of `slice(0, max)` — `decisions.jsonl`, `decisions/<date>-<slug>.md`, and the claim line, diagnostics report, activity-feed label and browser text beside them — so the stored record round-trips through UTF-8 as the whole character instead of U+FFFD.
