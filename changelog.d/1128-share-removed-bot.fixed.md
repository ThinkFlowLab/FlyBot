**Share with a friend** used to report success for a Bot the other home no longer runs. A row whose Bot is gone from the roster that home is still listed in now says so — `<Bot> was removed from that home. Sharing with it cannot reach anyone.` — and its **Share** is disabled; a click that still reaches the host is refused with `bot/removed-bot` and sends no grant, so the list stops claiming a share that would reach nobody. The row is judged on both the peer id and the handle (`list_remote_bots` entries carry both), and a home missing from the cached roster, or a roster read that failed or came back empty, decides nothing — it shares as before rather than guessing (#1128).
