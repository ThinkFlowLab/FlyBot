A read-only FlyBot window no longer rewrites `flybot-social-settings.json` from its read endpoints: `social/remote-bots` and `social/friends` still derive the inbound backfill, the hidden-remote aging and the accept-notice cleanup in memory, but they write nothing, so opening a second window cannot create that file, age a hidden friend on it, or put an older copy back over what the writable window saved ([#2231](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2231)).
