A timeline trace whose open is refused — a directory that cannot be listed, one removed underneath, or an I/O error — now gives its writer lock back instead of keeping it for the life of the process, so the next open in the same process and every later process succeeds once the problem is gone, and one transient read failure no longer leaves the on-disk trace switched off until a restart ([#2629](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2629)).
