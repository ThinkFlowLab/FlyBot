A failed card that can never succeed no longer offers **Retry** on any surface: the chat bubble and the dock now ask one shared predicate instead of reading only "failed", so a Bot that is gone or no longer shared, or a send refused as not permitted, shows the reason without a button that cannot work, while temporary failures (busy, rate-limited, timed out) keep theirs ([#968](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/968)).
