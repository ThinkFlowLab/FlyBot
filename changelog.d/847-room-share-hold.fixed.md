A friend's Bot added to a local group chat now reaches that friend's FlyBot with no approval card: the host clears Bot Social's blanket hold on the room's own `FLYBOT-ROOM-SHARE` envelope (the same allow-list a face-room line uses), so the invited home sees the shared room as soon as the Bot is added and each later group line goes out with it. A hold with a finding of its own keeps its card, and a card that does appear reads one plain sentence (the invite, or the group message) instead of the raw envelope and its local ids ([#847](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/847)).
