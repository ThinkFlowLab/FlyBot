A Bot that only shares a room with a friend's Bot can message it again: both send paths name the room on `send_as_member` when the two Bots are live in one face room this home has not left, so Bot Social decides on the room-scoped reach the two already hold instead of answering that they are not friends. Lines outside a room, into a local room, and after leaving the room name no room, and a room-scoped refusal now reads as the room's own sentence ("that room is closed between these Bots now…", "the room no longer lets these Bots reach each other…") instead of "you are not friends with this Bot" ([#881](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/881), [#866](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/866)).
