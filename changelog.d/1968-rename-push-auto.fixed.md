Renaming a Bot now reaches the guests already seated in every unnamed group it sits in ([#1968](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1968)): the host recomputed the room's title from its members and retitled the room locally, but pushed nothing, so a shared group kept its old title on the other homes until the next chat line and a quiet group kept it indefinitely. The automatic rename now rides the same one `join` an explicit rename already used (#1949) and carries the new name, while a rename that leaves the computed title alone — a Bot outside the group, a group with a chosen name, a group with no remote guest — still sends nothing.
