When sharing a Bot with a friend fails, the Sharing card keeps that message as the share's own: it no longer offers a **Try again** that only reloads the friend list, and a later successful reload no longer wipes it. The friend-list read keeps its retry and its empty state, and the two errors no longer stand in for each other ([#987](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/987)).
