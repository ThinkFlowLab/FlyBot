A line that arrives from another home with half of a surrogate pair in its body now has the body made well formed before it is used: the stored room line, the line the pane draws and the text handed to the Bot that was called all carry the replacement character instead of a lone half that would not survive a UTF-8 round trip. The line is still accepted and still calls that Bot — a broken character never silences a peer's message — and a body that is already well formed, including one with a real emoji, is passed through exactly as it arrived ([#1943](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1943)).
