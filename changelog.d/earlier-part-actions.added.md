The messages a Bot's chat loads above the current part are now messages you can act on: the reaction and reply buttons on a bubble from an earlier part used to do nothing, because the bubble was drawn without the identity a reaction is stored under and without a session to quote into. A reaction picked on such a bubble now sticks to that message and stays there when another page is loaded above it, and **Reply** quotes the line into the open chat instead of into the part it was said in. ([#2136](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2136)).
