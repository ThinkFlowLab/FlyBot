Bot Social's Sharing and friend cards now speak the window's language: the five headings and lines from [#2973](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2973) plus the accept-invite submit, the Retry on a send that failed, the open-room chip and the invite link button now draw the Chinese `core/src/client/i18n.js` already carried, instead of English. Text a user or a Bot stored — a friend's name, a send error — is still shown exactly as stored, never translated.
