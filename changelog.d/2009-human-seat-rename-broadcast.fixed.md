Adding or removing a person in a group without a chosen name now tells the guests already seated the new name ([#2009](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2009)): before this a human seat moved the host's own name and Session title while the other homes kept the copy they arrived with, because only a Bot's seat change sent the one `join` a guest holds. A group with a chosen name, a mirror of another home's room and a face-to-face room still send nothing for a seat change.
