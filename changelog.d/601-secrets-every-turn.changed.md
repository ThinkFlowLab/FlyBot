Every one of your own Bots now gets your saved keys (such as `$DSH_SECRET_GITHUB_TOKEN`) on every turn: group rounds, Bot-to-Bot messages, scheduled turns, turns of a Bot you share with a friend, and commands a Bot runs in a sub-agent. A friend's Bot still never gets them, and a key you set to Chosen Bots still goes only to those Bots ([#601](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/601)).
