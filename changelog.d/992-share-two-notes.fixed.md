The Sharing card now shows both facts at once: a failed **Share** and a failed friend-list read each keep their own message, so clicking Share no longer erases the list error and its **Try again**, and the five-second refresh no longer erases what the share told you. A successful refresh settles only the list error, and a successful share settles only its own line ([#992](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/992)).
