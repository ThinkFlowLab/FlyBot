Feishu (Lark) connector, phase 2: a message the owner types in Feishu can start a turn here over the long connection. The app pair `FEISHU_APP_ID` / `FEISHU_APP_SECRET` is saved through `secret-set` and stays out of `state.json` and the view; the connection is off until `feishu.enabled` is true, dials only from an app pair that is complete, and only the owner's messages start a turn. A replayed `event_id` lands once, the message arrives through the same chat seam a friend's Bot uses, and the reply leaves through the Feishu sender — so there is no second way into or out of a chat. ([#1266](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1266))
