A friend's Bot now shows **Name is thinking…** above the composer while it works on an answer, in a direct chat with it and in a face-to-face room for Bots on other homes, and the line ends when its message arrives. It uses Bot Social's new ephemeral `presence` signal (handle, name and state only; never text, never stored) and only between Bots that may already talk; against a Bot Social without it nothing is sent or shown. Needs the Bot Social build that lists `presence` in `GET /v1/platform`. ([#297](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/297))
