A Bot that names another member with `@Name` now gets that member called in the shape that used to lose the call: a line posted into the group while a round is running calls the members it names inside that same round (under the usual per-member and per-round caps, exactly once — the re-delivered line calls nobody again), and a late line after the round limit still calls the member it names instead of letting `quiet` swallow the name while the admin answers. A name outside the group, the sender's own name, and a member's failure or stop notice still call nobody ([#932](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/932)).
