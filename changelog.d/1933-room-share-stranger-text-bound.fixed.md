A joined room's notice and the pane line copied from another owner's FlyBot keep whole characters ([#1933](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1933); [#1928](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1928) covered the envelopes, and these are the two host-side readers it left on a raw code-unit cut): the guest bounds the notice it stores at its own 2,000 units and the history line it draws at the same 2,000-unit bound it already stores, both by backing up to a code point boundary, so a text whose 2,000th unit is half of a surrogate pair no longer leaves that half in the notice or in the pane — it reads as U+FFFD no more. The pane used to be shown the sender's raw `entry.text`, longer than the line the guest's own log kept; it now draws exactly the bounded prefix the log holds.
