A join to a shared room keeps the group notice even when the room's Bot roster has to shrink to fit ([#1932](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1932)): the roster used to be trimmed after the notice, and the notice was cut by the whole envelope's overflow, so a room with enough Bots emptied it and a guest never heard an edited or cleared notice — an explicit clear (an empty notice) was dropped along with it. The roster now gives its rows first, the notice is cut only by what is left over once the roster cannot give more, and a present empty notice arrives as the clear it is. No limit changes: a join still fits Bot Social's 4,000-character transport.
