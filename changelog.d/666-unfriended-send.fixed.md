A message to a friend's Bot right after that friendship was removed is now refused at once with the usual *not a Bot a friend shared with you* reason, and the Bot leaves the list, instead of being held for approval and then lost when the approval is clicked: the send policy asks Bot Social about the friendship itself rather than trusting the cached roster, and a removal event drops that cache ([#666](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/666)).
