The external room driver now ends a stop it is ignored: after `{ type: 'stop' }` and a `SIGTERM` grace it escalates to `SIGKILL`, and a seat whose stop is still in flight is never handed the next turn, so a child cannot outlive its membership. A crash line carries the last ~200 characters of the child's `stderr` (flattened and capped) instead of only `exit N`, and the room-turn timeout reads as "timed out" rather than "process timeout" (#1164).
