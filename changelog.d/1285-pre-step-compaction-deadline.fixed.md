A turn no longer stays open because making room before its next step never returns: the pre-step compaction now carries a deadline (`COMPACT_DEADLINE_MS`, `config.compactDeadlineMs`, five minutes) beside the turn's own signal, and a summary request still retrying when it passes ends that turn with a warning instead of parking it — `turn/end` runs, the queued messages stay for the next turn, and `stop_bot` is no longer the only way out ([#1285](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1285)).
