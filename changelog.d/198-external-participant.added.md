A Bot can be spoken for by a supervised external process in rooms (#198): `create_bot` takes an `external` block (`driver: subprocess`, an argv array, optional `cwd` and `env`), and `room-participant.js` routes that member to a new driver while every other member keeps its DSH Session. The process is started per room and Bot, answers one JSON line per turn, and its start, clean exit, crash and timeout are explicit states — a crash is posted as the Bot's own line in the room, and a turn that reaches a dead process ends with a failure instead of silence.
