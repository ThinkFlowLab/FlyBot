A line a friend typed themselves to one of your Bots (for example to your Helper, through their Chief) now shows as that person, with their name and a person mark, instead of as their Bot. Bot Social attests the line as before; the chat now draws it as the person whenever it is attested, not only in your own chat with that friend's Bot. A line the Bot wrote still shows as the Bot.
