Project and roster ids resolve only from their table's own keys: `project-read {id: "__proto__"}`, `project-bind {kind: "bot", id: "__proto__"}` and `project-of` no longer answer from `Object.prototype`, a binding request can no longer write `projectId` onto it (which made every unbound Bot and group report one Project until the next restart), and `findBot` / `findRooms` stop returning it as a member or a group ([#1704](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1704)).
