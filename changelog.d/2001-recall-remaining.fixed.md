`recall` now says when it cut its hit list ([#2001](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2001)): a keyword search with more than 30 matches ends with `N more entries match; narrow the query or read a topic` and the exact count (`1 more entry matches; …` when exactly one is left out), and a `semantic: "true"` search asks the index for one entry past the cap and ends with `more entries match this meaning than are shown; narrow the query or read a topic`. Before this, a Bot with 35 matching entries was shown 30 and told nothing, so entry 31 read as not existing. The cap stays 30 and a reply that shows every match adds no line.
