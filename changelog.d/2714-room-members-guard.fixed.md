A saved team whose group-chat record carries no `members` list (a hand edit, a restore from another machine, or a version-1 home from before the field) no longer makes FlyBot fail to start: the record's list is repaired to empty when the team is loaded, the room itself is kept, and the repaired team is saved, instead of every later load rejecting with no warning and no way to repair it from the UI. See [#2714](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2714).
