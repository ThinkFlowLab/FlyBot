`plugins/social/client.js`: a Bot Social hold whose send failed now keeps a **Not sent** card, with the reason and **Retry**, until it leaves the queue — the dock remembers the failure itself instead of only the one error line the next click clears, so the 5 s poll that still lists the hold as pending can no longer draw it back as waiting. Its **Retry** sends the same approval again (`approve`), once per card, and a second click on a card that already went out sends nothing. Local failed holds keep their existing **Not sent** / **Retry** behavior.
