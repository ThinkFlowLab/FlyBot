`AGENTS.md` no longer gives a Main Bot a removal right it has no tool for: it gets `create_bot` and `update_bot`, `stop_bot` only ends a running turn, and deleting a Bot stays the person's action in the UI (`delete-bot`). The team paragraph now says that, and points at [#638](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/638) for the open decision on a retire tool ([#841](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/841)).
