The gate-change signature is a job of its own (`policy-sign`) instead of a step inside the `Test` suite, and `main`'s ruleset file names it next to `tests-ok` and `enqueue-guard`: required contexts are job names, so deleting the step used to leave the suite green, while the suite now needs the signature job and cannot run without it — a pull request that removes it loses a required context instead of passing. The live ruleset still has to be given the same context, which is the owner's edit: until then the job reports without being required ([#2124](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2124)).
