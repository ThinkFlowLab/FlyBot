A Workflow no longer keeps a task forever: when the card's own fields later name another live Workflow or local Bot, its stored claim and route are dropped and the card is matched again, so the task moves instead of staying on the old target. Re-ingesting the same target is still a no-op, and a trigger or a card that names nothing leaves the claim alone. The Workflows panel now lists, per Workflow, the task ids it holds (bounded to 20 ids plus a count) and gives each one a Re-match action that drops just that claim through the existing ingest call, with no second polling loop. ([#1647](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1647))
