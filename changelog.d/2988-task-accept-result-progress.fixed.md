Accepting a result in the Inbox now finishes the card at 100% like a board move to Done: the card used to keep the number it last stored (60% on a card returned at 60%), so the same finished card read 100% from the board and 60% from the Inbox, and an accepted card could not be corrected afterwards. A cancelled or failed card still keeps its last number. See [#2988](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2988).
