A Bot turn the provider refused with a requests-per-window `429` (`Limit type: requests`, `Remaining: 0`) is now asked again after a backoff — 2 s, 8 s and 30 s — instead of spending the turn, and the group round or sender keeps waiting for the same exchange. A refusal that outlasts every attempt ends the turn once, in the visible failure line the chat already draws, naming the provider text. A budget or quota refusal is not asked again ([#1308](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1308)).
