`workflow/assign` now delivers the brief it is given. The brief is stored on the task, kept on a parked card (so `workflows.json` and `workflow/state` show what the node was actually told) and handed to the node as its instruction, with the title used only when no brief is given; `run_workflow` treats a brief the same way and takes its title from the brief's first line, and a field `assign` does not read is now named back in `ignored` instead of being dropped in silence ([#1869](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1869)).
