A group that a friend's Bot joined through an invite link now reaches that guest instead of asking Bot Social about a friendship the two homes never had: the seat the link admitted is the one pair the link opened, read from the seat itself or from the link's own record of who it admitted, so a group message or update goes out under the room rather than sitting on an approval that could not be approved. A Bot added to the group by hand is not a link pair and is unchanged ([#923](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/923)).
