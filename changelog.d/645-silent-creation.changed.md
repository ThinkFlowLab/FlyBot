Making a Bot or a group chat no longer starts a turn: a new Bot is sent no greeting and waits in the sidebar until you write to it or a parent Bot messages it, a new group posts no opening round, and picking a Bot's model applies it without speaking to the Bot. Text you type in the composer before creating a Bot is sent to it as your first message, so it answers you, and it is never saved as the Bot's instructions; making a team costs no model turn ([#645](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/645)).
