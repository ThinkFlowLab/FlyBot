A write the host refuses in the details drawer's own Settings view now says so above the page instead of at the top of the scrolling box: pressing **Pin**, **Hide**, **Make admin** or **Remove** at the bottom of a group's page wrote `bot/read-only` above the viewport, where the card and its switches never changed and the press looked ignored ([#2385](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2385)). The refusal line is drawn as a sibling above the scrolling body, the shape the Settings dialog has used since #2194, and a write that succeeds draws no line.
