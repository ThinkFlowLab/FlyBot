A read-only window's Inbox now keeps every row and its **Open** action and draws no write the host would refuse: no answer field or **Submit** on a question or a task input, and no **Accept** or **Send back** on a result. One sentence at the top of the dialog carries the host's own read-only words, the same hint the other read-only surfaces draw. An approval keeps **Reject** and **Allow**, because `approval-answer` is one of the reads a read-only channel allows, and a writable window draws exactly the tree it drew before.
