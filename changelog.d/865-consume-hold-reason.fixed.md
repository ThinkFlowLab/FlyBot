Auto-send decides on Bot Social's `hold_reason` instead of guessing from `reasons` ([#865](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/865)): the two outbound paths clear only `blanket_outbound`, and `risk`, `attachment`, `policy` and any value this build does not know keep their card for the owner. The face-room path also clears `face_room_auto_send_off`, which is nothing but that Bot's own room Auto-send switch on a clean line, so the switch the owner turned on keeps sending ([#457](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/457)). A server that does not send the field keeps the old rule, and when the field and `reasons` disagree the field wins.
