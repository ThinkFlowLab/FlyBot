A wire between two cards on one row of a workflow flowchart — a loop across a parallel layer, or a skip across a row — now runs outside that row instead of through the card between them: the run fell back onto the row's own column and the card, drawn on top, covered it, so the stroke looked like a link to the neighbour. The two horizontal runs now sit in the space beyond the row's own cards, and the vertical run between them keeps clear of every card it passes, the two ends included ([#2151](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2151)).
