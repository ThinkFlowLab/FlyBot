A room-share envelope keeps whole characters in the text it copies to another owner's FlyBot ([#1928](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1928)): the history line a join copies (bounded at 2,000 units on both the host that builds the envelope and the guest that copies it) and a line fitted to Bot Social's 4,000-character transport limit now stop before a character that straddles their bound instead of leaving half of it, so a guest no longer reads U+FFFD where the sender typed an emoji — a family emoji fitted to the transport reproduces the old break. The 18,000-unit bound on a line envelope's body goes through the same rule for consistency; it is not reachable on its own because the transport fit already shortens the body to 4,000.
