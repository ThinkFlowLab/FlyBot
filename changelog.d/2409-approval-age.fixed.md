An approval card now says how long its request has waited. The dock card and the held reply's bubble read `Waiting for approval · 12 min`, and a shared room's overview rows carry the same age beside the request's text; the four existing strings in `text.js` `relativeShort()` do the wording, so a wait under a minute reads `just now` and one that crossed midnight falls back to a date. A card whose record carries no `createdAt` draws nothing extra ([#2409](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2409)).
