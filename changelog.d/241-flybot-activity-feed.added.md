The `flybot` service has a team activity feed: plugins subscribe through `ctx.flybot.on('turn.start' | 'turn.end' | 'tool.start' | 'tool.end' | 'exchange.delivered')` to see Bot turns, tool calls and Bot-to-Bot deliveries without touching raw session events. The feed carries ids, times and outcomes only, never message text, tool arguments or output. It is the event source for the upcoming `@flybot/timeline` plugin ([#241](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/241)).
