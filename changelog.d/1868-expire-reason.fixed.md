A Workflow step that runs past its lane's `stepTimeoutMinutes` is now reported as having reached its deadline (`step deadline reached (45 min)`) instead of `no reply for 45 minutes`, so a step that was still working no longer reads as a Bot that went silent; the stage line and `run.failure` carry that wording, and the `define_workflow` tool accepts `stepTimeoutMinutes` (1–120) so a new lane does not silently inherit the 45-minute default ([#1868](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1868)).
