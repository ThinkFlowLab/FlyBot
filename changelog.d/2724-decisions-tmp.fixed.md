The decision log no longer shares one temp file between writers: the trim stages the retained log at `decisions.jsonl.<pid>.<uuid>.tmp` and renames it over the log, so two hosts of one team folder cannot take each other's temp away and report a write that never landed — the `a decision was not written: … ENOENT …` warning, and the trim that was lost with it, are gone ([#2724](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2724)). A read-only window, the second host of one team folder, now writes nothing at all: `record_decision` there appends no line, trims nothing, fires no `decision` event, and answers `Not recorded: this window is read-only, …` instead of claiming a record that is not on disk.
