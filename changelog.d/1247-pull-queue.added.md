A group's board can now be a pull queue: the lead enqueues an item for a role (`track_tasks` with `queue` and `role`, no assignee), an idle member of that role claims it with `claim` and one item is handed to exactly one member, and a member that cannot finish returns it with a one-line reason and a `retryAt`; the room board shows one line per role with its depth and oldest wait ([#1247](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1247)).
