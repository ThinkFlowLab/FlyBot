The invite card now strips untrusted fence markers from a room's `name` and `lead` until the string stops changing, as the server's `stripFaceMarkers` does. One pass could remove a match and assemble a new one — a nested `<untrusted-<untrusted-filemessage>` lost its inner match and left a complete `<untrusted-message>` behind — so a remote room could put marker text into the card a person reads ([#822](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/822)).
