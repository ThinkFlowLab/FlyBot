A human message sent while a foreground tool or job wait has been open for more than 60 seconds now stops that turn the way **Stop** does and keeps the message in the inbox, so the line is claimed at once instead of waiting for the command to finish. Shorter waits, finished waits, group rooms and idle Bots are untouched ([#1509](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1509)).
