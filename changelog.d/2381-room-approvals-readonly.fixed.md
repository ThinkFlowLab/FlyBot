A window that holds the team read-only no longer draws **Approve** or **Reject** on a shared room's "Waiting for your approval" card in the details drawer: a decision is a write, so the host refuses it there and the two buttons could only produce a refusal after the click. The card keeps its headline, its count and each request's own text, and says why in the host's own sentence. A decision the host still refuses — in a writable window, or in a room with several members — now writes that reason on the card in one alert line instead of disappearing ([#2381](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2381)).
