A Workflow row with live runs no longer replaces its accessible name with `{name} · {count} running`. The count rides in a visually hidden span beside the `aria-hidden` dot instead, so a reader gets everything the row's content gives — the lane's name, the `Workflow` badge, its Project and its preview — plus how many runs are live. Two lanes that share a name are distinguishable again, and a read-only window still hears the count (#2230).
