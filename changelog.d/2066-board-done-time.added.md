A task card in the Done column now says when it finished — a relative time (`2 h`, `Sep 22`) beside the assignee, on a Bot's board and a group's alike ([#2066](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2066)). The moment is the first terminal entry in the card's history (`accepted`, `cancelled` or `failed`), so editing a finished task no longer moves the time; a card recorded before that history existed falls back to its last update, and one with no usable stamp shows no time at all.
