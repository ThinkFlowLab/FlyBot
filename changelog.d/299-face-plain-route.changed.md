Face-to-face rooms: a plain line (no @) is no longer answered by every Bot. Each Bot now decides whether the line is for it: a specific question gets one answer, a follow-up goes to the Bot that spoke last, and a line to everyone ("大家介绍一下自己") may get a short answer from each. A Bot that passes posts nothing. If every Bot passes on your own line, the first Bot answers so you are never ignored. `@Name` and `@all` are unchanged, replies' own `@`s still start nothing, and approval and Auto-send rules are unchanged.
