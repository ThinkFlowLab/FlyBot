A Bot with unfinished conversation tasks keeps working without a new message. The host starts a continue turn for the next task, in that Bot's own chat or in the group session that owns the subtask, and stops when the work is done or waiting on an answer. In a group, members with their own subtasks are woken in the same pass ([#1109](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1109)).
