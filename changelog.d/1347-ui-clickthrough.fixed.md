The Bot settings drawer opens wide enough for its own controls: at 320px the "Name" field's label broke into "Nam" and "e", the "Test connection" button into "Test connecti" and "on", and Main Bot's "Change" into "Ch", "an", "ge". The Details panel now opens at 380px, a row's label stays on one line, a button never breaks its own words, and below 300px — the panel's floor in a narrow window — the row stacks so nothing is clipped to fit a width that is not there. A preview in the sidebar shows the words of a line and never its untrusted fence or the fragment an older write left behind (`from="User"> …`). A Bot chat with nothing said yet, which the shell draws with no header at all, now names itself above the composer and says it is empty instead of being a blank column with a composer. The details drawer's copy button is named for what it does ("Copy conversation", not "Share conversation") and says "Conversation copied as Markdown" when the copy lands, or reports why it did not. The mode word beside the chat title no longer draws as a filled chip that looks clickable and is not. A provider failure stored as a cut-off JSON body — a real Inbox showed `404: {"code":"UnsupportedModel","message":"The requested model does not support …` — now reads as a sentence naming the feature and where to change the model, in the chat's failure lines and in the Inbox ([#1347](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1347)). A chat message can no longer paint over a dialog, a sheet or a scrim. The chat column had no stacking context of its own, so anything the browser gave its own composited layer — a new row's entrance animation, a filter, a sticky box — was ordered against the page root instead of against the column, and could sort above a dialog's layer. With the column made one stacking context that sits below the panel and the dialogs, a right-aligned message stays behind **Tasks**, **Settings** and the **Inbox** scrim; measured on a live page with a promoted bubble, chat content inside the Tasks dialog went from 1842 painted pixels to none. Dialogs and popovers also moved to separate tiers (dialogs 120/121, popovers 130, palette 140), because the context menu and the reaction strip shared z-index 60 with the Settings dialog and a later-opened popover could paint over it ([#1347](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1347)).
 A modal also covers what is behind it now: the scrim was translucent enough that the thread stayed legible through it, and each overlay layer is its own stacking context so the scrim and its dialog move as one ([#1347](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1347)).
