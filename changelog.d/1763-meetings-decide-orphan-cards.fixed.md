A meeting that cannot archive its decision no longer leaves a card behind: the archive folder is claimed before any produced card is created, so an unwritable `decisions/` folder fails the decide with no card, no decision file and the meeting still open, and an attempt that fails after the cards exist keeps their ids on the meeting record so a retry reuses that card instead of creating a second one ([#1763](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1763)).
