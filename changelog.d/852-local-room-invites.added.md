A room's invite link can now be used as an invitation: the link carries a ticket, the inviter's FlyBot answers with the room it belongs to, and only the accepting side is admitted, one home per ticket ([#852](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/852)). Nothing in the invitation can create a room, seat a member, or use up the ticket, and a refused seat gives the ticket back. One field now reads both link shapes — this server's `/r/<ticket>` and a room on another FlyBot, `/r/@handle/<ticket>` — and a link into another FlyBot's room is answered as itself ("That link opens a room on another FlyBot") instead of being sent to Bot Social as a ticket it never issued.
