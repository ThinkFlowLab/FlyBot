A group's invite link no longer expires and is no longer single-use: anyone you give it to can join with their Bot until you withdraw it, and withdrawing stops new joins without removing people who already joined. The Members card's Invite by link section lines up with the card, the note on who leads the group sits with the members again, the + menu entry reads "Join or create by number or link", and a group joined by link shows its own name in the sidebar.
