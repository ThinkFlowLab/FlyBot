`read_group_chat`'s `newest N of M` no longer shrinks when a co-seat mention's booking sits below the reader's join floor: the count now discounts only the bookings at or above that floor, so a Bot that joined after a busy room's bookings are told the lines it can see (`newest 30 of 211` instead of `newest 30 of 201`) rather than a total short by one per mention.
