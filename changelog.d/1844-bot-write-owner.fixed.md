A Main Bot can no longer rewrite, retire or stop another owner's Bot. On a deployment where the owner rule is enforced, `update_bot`, `archive_bot` and `stop_bot` now accept a target only when it belongs to the Bot's own owner or is granted to that owner — the same rule `list_bots` and the HTTP side already kept — so the instructions, retirement and running turns of another owner's Bot stay untouched ([#1844](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1844)). The three refusals also read one sentence, `No Bot named <name>.`, whether the Bot is missing or foreign, and no longer name the rest of the team. Single-owner installs behave exactly as before, and the refusal text is aligned with no other behaviour change.
