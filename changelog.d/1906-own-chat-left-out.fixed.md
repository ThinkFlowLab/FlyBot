`read_own_chat` now says what a cap left out instead of reading as the whole chat: a search that fills its 20 hits ends with `N more not shown; search with narrower words, or read around one of these anchors.`, and a bare read of a part with more than 12 messages is headed `newest 12 of 15, oldest first`. The caps stay; exactly at the cap nothing is added. ([#1906](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1906))
