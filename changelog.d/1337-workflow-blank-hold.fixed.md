The workflow planner no longer reads a blank hold as a hold. A hold now names its holder or it is no hold at all: `''` — the shape a pull-request query that answered nothing arrives in — and `'   '` plan exactly like no hold, and a hold no name can be read from is treated as no hold instead of refusing every node of a job with either no sentence or nobody's name. A real holder is unchanged: every card-less node is still refused, with that name and the definition's own hold sentence. ([#1337](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1337))
