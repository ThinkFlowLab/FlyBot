A meeting is now a record on disk instead of a decoration of the live process: every write of the `meeting` tool (`open`, `position`, `decide`, `block`, `resume`) reaches the team file before the tool answers, so reopening the app shows the meeting in the state the caller was told — the `core-host` contract that a meeting survives a restart now holds for every write verb. A decided meeting stays `decided` — a retry made by a reader that saw it as `deliberating` is refused with `closed` instead of writing a second `decisions/…-2.md` and a second card — and an open meeting keeps its recorded positions. A decision also saves the ids of the cards it produced before anything further is written, so a restart between the cards and the archived file keeps those cards instead of doubling them ([#1774](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1774)).
