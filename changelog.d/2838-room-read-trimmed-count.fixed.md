`read_group_chat` now counts the lines the room said, not only the lines its log still holds: a room that said 500 lines and keeps the newest 60 reads `Latest in "Ops" (newest 30 of 500, oldest first):` instead of `newest 30 of 60`, and a reader at floor line 300 reads `newest 30 of 201`. A room that never trimmed reads byte-identically, and the caps (`ROOM_READ_MAX` 30, `ROOM_LOG_MAX` 60, the membership store's 200) are unchanged.
