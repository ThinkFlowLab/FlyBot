A room shared into a FlyBot home is now named after the person who invited it, not after the Bot that carried the invitation across Bot Social. The receiving side reads the inviter from the share envelope (`inviterName` / `inviterHome`) and falls back to the transport only when the envelope names nobody, so the room reads **Shared room from Alice** instead of **Shared room from Chief**. A projection recorded under the transport's identity before this fix is still recognised as the same room rather than appearing twice, and chat lines keep their own speaker ([#873](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/873)).
