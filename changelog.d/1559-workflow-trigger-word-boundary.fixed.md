Workflow auto-assign now matches an ASCII trigger only as a whole word, so `notice` no longer routes a card that merely says `noticeable`, and `pr` no longer routes `Improve`, `Prepare` or `Sprint`. The whole word still routes (`notice`, `pr`), and non-ASCII triggers such as `周报` keep matching inside a longer title. The two-character trigger guard is unchanged.
