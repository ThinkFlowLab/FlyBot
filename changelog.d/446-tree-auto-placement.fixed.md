Bots made outside the tree (Chief's `create_bot`, the Bot list) now always join the tree under the same `maxChildren` and `maxDepth` limits as `create_child_bot`. When a level is full, the tree reorganizes itself instead of leaving the Bot outside while the sidebar showed it under Chief: the Bot goes under the best-matching Bot, matching siblings are grouped under a lead, or it moves one level down. A parent left over the limit after a deletion is repaired the same way. Bots are only moved, never deleted, and every move is logged ([#446](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/446)).
