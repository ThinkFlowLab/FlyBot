A tree reorganization no longer waits for ever on a host call that never answers: after 10 minutes the job is abandoned with its id in the log (`flybot-tree: reorganization job <id> was abandoned after …`), later placements, repairs and the daily tidy still run, and `settled()` still resolves. An abandoned `exclusive()` job reports `abandoned: true` with its job id instead of hanging. See [#2751](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2751).
