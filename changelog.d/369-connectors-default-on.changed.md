Connector tools (GitHub, Composio) are now **on by default for you** in every Bot, new and existing, and a Bot you share (a grant, a friend, a face room, a room code) stays usable by you with them. The default-off setting moved to **other people**: when a friend, another room member or another owner's Bot drives a Bot, or a Bot works in a room with other people in it, the tools are off unless you turn on that Bot's separate **also when others use it** switch, which sharing no longer disables (Settings → Connectors → **Choose Bots**, and each Bot's settings; zh copy included). Who is driving a turn is read from the messages the turn took in when each tool is called, and anything that cannot be determined counts as someone else's. A Bot post into a room (and the report that comes back) carries the verdict of the turn that made it, so a friend driving a Main Bot cannot reach your connectors through the members of an owner-only room. After upgrading, Bots that #346 left off are on again; an explicit off you set stays off, and Disconnect resets every Bot's choices. This replaces #346's "off by default, never on a shared Bot" rule; the Composio session address still stays out of `state.json` ([#369](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/369), [#335](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/335)).
