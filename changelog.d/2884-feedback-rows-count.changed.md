The Feedback page's error list now says how much of it is drawn: the newest eight rows sit under a count like `Showing 8 of 50`, and the existing `Show more` control opens the rest, with the line reading `Showing all 50` while it is open. A reader with more errors than fit can now tell the list is cut rather than complete before quoting it in a bug report; eight or fewer errors keep the list exactly as it was, with no count and no control. See [#2884](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2884).
