The run flowchart now routes every wire instead of drawing one symmetric cubic per edge: each wire leaves a port on its source card's bottom edge, crosses the gap between rows in its own lane, and lands on a port of its target card's top edge, so parallel and back edges no longer stack into a single stroke or cross a card. An edge whose target sits above its source is drawn as a dashed loop around the cards, and the dashed parallel frame now takes equal margins from the measured card boxes instead of the authored 72px row height ([#2122](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/2122)).
